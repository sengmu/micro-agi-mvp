# Third-party components and artwork

This prototype uses Android/AndroidX, Kotlin, Chaquopy, Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter, multimult, PIXI, pixi-live2d-display and Live2D Cubism Core. Dependency coordinates are in the Gradle files. Existing notices in bundled scripts and pixi-unsafe-eval.LICENSE are retained.

The Hiyori model and Live2D Cubism Core are omitted as standalone files from the public source repository and remain third-party components of the functional prototype APK. Their applicable licenses and redistribution conditions must be confirmed before publicly redistributing the model/runtime or publishing to a store. The source staging folder is not proof of license clearance. No new blanket license is granted over those components or the existing artwork.

Room artwork, character images and font files retain their original rights. Verify their provenance and usage terms before publication. Replace any asset whose distribution permission cannot be established.
